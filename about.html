About

Sheila & Lisa.

A doctor and a nurse who run their practice together on Middle Neck Road, Great Neck.

Natural, from the inside out

Sheila Omrani and Lisa Farazmand are a doctor-and-nurse duo who share a passion for natural, inside-out approaches to anti-aging, longevity and overall wellness.

Their mission is to help people look and feel their best every day, through proactive, preventative care and healthy-aging practices designed to support long-term vitality and well-being.

The practice

One address on Middle Neck Road, where the aesthetic side and the wellness side of the practice are run by the same two people. Skin, hair, longevity and IV therapy are all planned in the same room.

Preventative, not reactive

Care here starts before there is something to correct. Blood work is reviewed before a longevity protocol begins. Neurotoxin is dosed conservatively and looked at again two weeks later. Microneedling and hair treatments are planned as a course, because that is how they are studied.

If a treatment is not right for you, you will be told that at the consultation rather than after it.
