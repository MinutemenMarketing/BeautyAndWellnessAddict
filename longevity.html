Longevity & wellness

The years
ahead.

Preventative wellness, planned over years rather than weeks.

Most people wait for a symptom.

Conventional care responds to problems. That is necessary, and it starts late. We ask a different question: what is already drifting, and what can be supported now so it does not become something else later?

The answer is rarely one product. Usually it is a small number of consistent things, done in the right order, reviewed often enough to notice a change.
