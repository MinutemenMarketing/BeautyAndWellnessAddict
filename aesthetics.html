Aesthetics & skin

Skin health first.

Treatments chosen for how skin behaves, not for how it looks in one photograph.

How we treat skin

Assess before treating. Sometimes the first recommendation is to change what you use at home.

Sequence, not stacking. More in one visit is rarely better and often worse.

Restraint by default. The smallest intervention that achieves what you asked for.

Regenerative

The slower treatments, and the ones most worth planning as a course. They work with the skin's own repair rather than sitting on top of it.

Microneedling

Controlled micro-injury that prompts collagen and repair. For texture, fine lines, pores and the appearance of scarring.

From $300

Microneedling with PRP

The same treatment with platelet-rich plasma prepared from your own blood on the day.

From $500

REJURAN

Polynucleotide preparation derived from salmon DNA, chosen where skin quality and resilience are the priority.

From $500

ZO firming / brightening serum

A targeted serum finish to microneedling, for uneven tone, dullness and early laxity.

From $360

PRP facial injections

Platelet-rich plasma injected where a regenerative approach suits better than added volume.

From $500

Resurfacing & maintenance

What keeps skin consistent between the bigger treatments, and what to time carefully around events and travel.

The Perfect Derma Peel

Medical-grade peel for tone, clarity, congestion and the appearance of pigmentation. Peeling follows a few days later.

From $250

HydraFacial

Cleanse, exfoliate, extract, hydrate. Signature covers the essentials; Deluxe adds targeted steps chosen on the day.

From $200

LED light therapy

Anti-aging or acne protocols in short sessions, often added to another treatment. An OMNILUX mask is available for home use.

From $40

Aesthetic medicine

Injectable treatment is mapped and quoted before anything is administered, so the dose and the cost are agreed in advance. Not appropriate for everyone.

Toxin treatment

Small, mapped doses that soften the movement behind expression lines. Priced by unit, so you pay for the dose you receive.

$12 / unit

Lip flip

A small dose along the upper lip for a change in shape without added volume. Modest and temporary by design.

$150

What to expect

You will know the downtime, the aftercare and what a treatment can and cannot do before you commit to it. Where the result depends on a course rather than a session, we say so at the first appointment, including what it costs in total.

Results differ between individuals and are never guaranteed.
