What IV therapy is here

IV therapy delivers fluid and nutrients directly into the bloodstream. What is in the bag, how much and how often are decided by a clinician after reviewing your history, the same way any other prescribed treatment is decided.

We make no claims about curing, preventing or treating disease with infusions, and IV therapy is not appropriate for everyone.

The visit

Four steps, about an hour.

01

Consult

History, medications and what you are hoping to feel differently. Suitability is decided here.

02

Customize

Ingredients and volume chosen for you, and explained before we start.

03

Drip

Forty-five minutes to an hour in the chair. Read, take a call, or sleep. Most people sleep.

04

Restore

Aftercare, hydration guidance, and a plan for whether and when to repeat.
